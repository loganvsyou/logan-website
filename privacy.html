Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Local Growth Systems collects and uses information submitted through this website. This is a starter policy and should be reviewed by a qualified professional before public launch.

Information collected

When someone requests a growth audit, this website may collect a name, business name, email address, phone number, service interest, website details, and any message submitted through the form.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to respond to inquiries, prepare website or lead generation recommendations, provide requested services, and improve the business process.

How information is shared

Local Growth Systems does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used for forms, email, analytics, customer relationship management, hosting, or project delivery.

Analytics and tracking

This website may use analytics tools to understand page visits, traffic sources, and form activity. Analytics should be configured in a privacy-conscious way before launch.

Lead gathering services

For clients who purchase lead gathering services, Local Growth Systems may process business contact data, prospect lists, campaign results, and reply data to deliver the contracted service. Campaigns should be configured to comply with applicable email and privacy laws.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to logan@localgrowthsystemsapp.com.
